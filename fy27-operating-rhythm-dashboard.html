The week

Seven core forums, with customer time, skip-levels, and transformation work scheduled around them. Times are a starting point; select any meeting to jump to its agenda.

Leadership rhythm Business transformation Prep and focus Customer and field
Every day: 7:45 morning scan (15 min) and 4:45 daily close (15 min). Morning scan · Daily close
Scheduled time budget
The remaining hours stay open for email, travel, and what breaks mid-week. Shift customer blocks up in the last month of a quarter.

Three questions for every major meeting

1. Where are we underutilizing capacity?

  • Reps, SDRs, SCs, CSMs, programs, customer relationships

2. What consumes capacity without producing revenue?

  • Low-yield meetings, unqualified opportunities, manual processes, low-converting plays

3. What will we stop, start, or reallocate?

  • Not another action item. A resource or operating decision.

Meeting playbook

Each meeting has one outcome, a timed agenda, and the inputs people bring. If the pre-read is not in by the night before, the meeting shrinks to the decision only.

Who decides what

The rhythm should not make you the center of every decision across 100+ people. Take exceptions; let the levels below own the rest.

FLM owns

  • Rep coaching and opportunity progression
  • Weekly execution
  • Normal deal obstacles

Director owns

  • Regional capacity and pipeline
  • Manager effectiveness
  • Forecast quality
  • Resource tradeoffs inside the region

You own

  • Cross-region allocation
  • Ecosystem prioritization
  • Structural productivity
  • Executive relationships and leadership bench
  • Exceptional revenue interventions

FY27 Revenue Productivity Index

North star: net new ASV per total revenue-organization FTE, with shared SDR, SC, CSM, Marketing, GTM, and Channel capacity allocated by one agreed rule. The other measures explain it. Read them side by side; do not add them.

Beyond the week

Benefit reviews

  • 30, 60, and 90 days after a transformation project scales.
  • Operational: hours saved per seller. Commercial: added meetings or qualified opportunities. Financial: incremental ASV against a comparison group.

Monthly

  • Revenue productivity bridge with Finance (first Tuesday).
  • Business review with the VP of Sales.
  • Win/loss and talent calibration sessions, scheduled when needed.
  • Detailed functional reviews with SDR, CSM, SC, GTM, Marketing, run by their leaders, not you.

Quarterly

  • Territory and capacity reset against yield and the Index.
  • Manager bench review: ready for director, needs a plan.
  • Customer advisory sessions with top accounts.

Retired from v1

  • Pipeline Council, SC sync, Attach and Expansion, Marketing and GTM sync: merged into the Revenue Growth Council.
  • Six weekly skip-levels cut to three.
  • Standing Talent and Win/Loss meetings: now monthly sessions when needed.

30-day pilot

  • Set baselines in week one, then run the cadence unchanged for 30 days.
  • Use the decisions-by-forum count on the Dashboard to see which meetings produced decisions.
  • Cut or merge any forum that produced none before adding anything new.

Rules for every meeting

  • Pre-read due the evening before.
  • One outcome, written in the invite.
  • Decision and owner logged within 30 minutes.
  • Forecast talk lives in Monday’s forum and the Finance outlook.
